## Before and after evidence

Before, the configured hours were arbitrary Polish-keyed display strings, the
UI parsed those strings and compared a closed sentinel, the timezone lived in
the utility, and the root route separately duplicated the schedule for
Schema.org.

After, one validated `OpeningSchedule` value owns all seven days, structured
slots, closed state, and timezone. The UI, open-now calculation, phone CTA, and
Schema.org metadata derive from it without duplicated production schedule
facts. The constructor recursively rejects extra authored keys and invalid
daily variants during typecheck, while unchecked runtime input is validated.
Configured zones must be present in the runtime's supported IANA location set,
so fixed offsets cannot silently bypass DST semantics. Exact public behavior
remains unchanged.

  its exact effect is to retain actual `@libs/openingHours` exports while
